# Status reports

Separate reported facts, computed results, risks or uncertainties, and recommendations. For each metric identify its event definition, numerator, denominator, period, unit, and source. Compute only when inputs align with the requested definition; otherwise state what is missing. Do not infer progress, completion, or ownership from silence. Keep the requested reporting period and audience in view.
