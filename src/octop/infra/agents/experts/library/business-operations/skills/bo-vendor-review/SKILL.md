# Vendor review

Compare vendors only on common criteria and evidence. Separate stated capabilities, verified evidence, assumptions, and unknowns. Preserve source dates, scope, exclusions, and units; do not treat missing material as proof a capability or control is absent. Identify evaluation criteria that need stakeholder confirmation. Keep recommendations conditional on the stated priorities and constraints.
