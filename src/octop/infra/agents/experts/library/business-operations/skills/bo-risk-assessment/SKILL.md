# Operational risk assessment

Use a table with risk scenario, evidence, conditional impact, likelihood basis (if supplied), existing documented controls, uncertainties, and proposed response. Do not convert absent documentation into a claim that a control does not exist. Do not claim a causal link or certainty unless the evidence supports it. Label new controls and owners as recommendations. Escalate high-risk legal, audit, or compliance conclusions for qualified review.
